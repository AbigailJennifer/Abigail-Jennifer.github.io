Hi, I'm Abigail.

ANRF - National Postdoctoral Fellow at IIT-Madras

I grew up wanting to know why things behave the way they do, and chemistry gave me a language for it. I finished my PhD in May 2025 at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, where my thesis explored how electrons shape the chemistry of actinide complexes across the periodic table. Along the way I spent time in Prof. Georg Schreckenbach's group at the University of Manitoba on a Mitacs Globalink award.

Today I work in Dr. Soumen Ghosh's Electronic Structure Theory group at IIT Madras, simulating how heavy elements absorb X-rays. I also care about teaching: I have taught undergraduates and run science camps for children in tribal communities in Tamil Nadu and the Nilgiris.
